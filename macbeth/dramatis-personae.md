# Macbeth — dramatis personae

This cast list is a reading index for the characters and groups named by the TEI source. The complete encoded cast list remains in [`macbeth.xml`](../macbeth.xml).

- The three Witches
- Duncan, King of Scotland
- Malcolm, Duncan’s son and successor
- Donalbain, Duncan’s son
- Macbeth, Thane of Glamis and Cawdor, later King of Scotland
- Lady Macbeth
- Banquo, Macbeth’s fellow captain
- Fleance, Banquo’s son
- Macduff, Thane of Fife
- Lady Macduff and her son
- Lennox, Ross, Angus, Menteith, and Caithness, Scottish nobles
- Siward, Earl of Northumberland, and Young Siward
- Hecate
- Murderers, soldiers, servants, messengers, doctors, gentlewoman, porter, captain, old man, lords, and attendants
